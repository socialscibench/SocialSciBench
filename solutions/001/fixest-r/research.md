# Baseline: fixest-r

Baseline: R fixest::feols (fixed-point demeaning with Irons-Tuck acceleration) via rpy2; reghdfe df_a and small-sample factor computed in the adapter. Threads: fixest nthreads = OMP_NUM_THREADS.

The adapter's docstring (`solution.py`) gives the exact call and the conversions it performs.

## Sources (accessed 2026-10-22)

- fixest 0.14.2, https://lrberge.github.io/fixest/ ; Berge, Butts & McDermott (2026), arXiv:2601.21749
