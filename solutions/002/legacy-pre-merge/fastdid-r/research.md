# Baseline: fastdid-r

Baseline: R fastdid (control_type dr, never-treated, varying base period) via rpy2, one call for group-time/event-study results and one for the simple ATT. No propensity-score trimming. Threads: data.table.

The adapter's docstring (`solution.py`) gives the exact call and the conversions it performs.

## Sources (accessed 2026-10-22)

- fastdid 1.0.6, https://tsailintung.github.io/fastdid/ (CRAN)
